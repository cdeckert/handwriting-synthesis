## Description

Turn typed text into realistic handwriting, entirely on your device.

Choose from 13 writing styles, format your text for popular paper and screen sizes, and export the result as SVG, PDF, PNG, or JPG.

Features:

- 13 distinct handwriting styles
- Live on-device preview
- A5, A4, A3, Letter, Legal, phone, tablet, desktop HD, and square layouts
- Portrait and landscape orientation
- Adjustable writing size and alignment
- Automatic word wrapping
- German umlauts, ß, and French accented letters
- Personal handwriting style capture with Apple Pencil on iPad
- SVG, PDF, PNG, and JPG export through the native share sheet
- No account, no ads, no tracking, and no network connection required

Your text and personal handwriting samples remain on your device.
