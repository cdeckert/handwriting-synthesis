# Privacy Policy for Handwriting Studio

Effective date: October 4, 2026

Handwriting Studio is designed to work entirely on your device. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

## Data processed by the app

- Text entered into the editor is processed locally on your device to generate handwriting.
- Personal handwriting styles created with Apple Pencil are stored locally on your device.
- Exported SVG, PDF, PNG, and JPG files are created locally. They leave the app only when you choose a destination through the system share sheet.

## Data collection and tracking

Handwriting Studio does not use analytics, advertising, third-party tracking, user accounts, or remote servers. It does not track you across apps or websites.

## Data retention and deletion

Editor content and personal handwriting styles remain on your device. You can remove personal handwriting styles in the app. Removing the app deletes its locally stored data, subject to your device and backup settings.

## Children’s privacy

Because the app does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before those changes take effect.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at [github.com/cdeckert/handwriting-synthesis/issues](https://github.com/cdeckert/handwriting-synthesis/issues).
